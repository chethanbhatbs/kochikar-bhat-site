What we do

Four areas of practice, handled from each of our three offices.

Dispute Resolution

Civil and criminal proceedings, from the first notice through trial and appeal.

  • Civil suits and recovery
  • Injunctions
  • Bail and trials
  • Appeals
02

Intellectual Property

Protection and enforcement of trademarks, copyright and designs.

  • Oppositions
  • Infringement

  • Appointment of arbitrators
  • Arbitral proceedings
  • Interim relief
  • Enforcement and challenge

Property transactions and property disputes.

  • Sale and lease agreements
  • Development agreements
  • Title and partition disputes
