Practice areas

What we do

Four areas of practice, handled from each of our offices.

  1. 01

    Dispute Resolution, Civil and Criminal

    Representation in civil and criminal proceedings, from the first notice through trial and appeal.

    • Civil suits, recovery and injunctions
    • Criminal complaints, bail and trials
    • Appeals and revisions
  2. 02

    Intellectual Property Rights

    Protection and enforcement of trademarks, copyright and designs.

    • Search, filing and registration
    • Oppositions and rectifications
    • Infringement and passing off
  3. 03

    Arbitration

    Domestic arbitration, and court proceedings that support or challenge it.

    • Notices and appointment of arbitrators
    • Conduct of arbitral proceedings
    • Interim relief, enforcement and challenges to awards
  4. 04

    Real Estate

    Property transactions and property disputes.

    • Title verification and due diligence
    • Sale, lease and development agreements
    • Partition, possession and title disputes
